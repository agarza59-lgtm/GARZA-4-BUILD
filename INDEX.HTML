Our Mission

Our mission is to protect endangered sea turtles and the environment they call home. We believe that even small actions can make a big difference in protecting marine wildlife.

How We Help

Turtle Haven Foundation works to make a difference through education, conservation, and community involvement.

Some ways to help include

  • Organizing beach cleanup events.
  • Educating communities about ocean pollution
  • Supporting sea turtle rescue programs
  • Protecting nesting areas along coastlines.
  • Encouraging responsible recycling habits.

Why Sea Turtles Matter

Sea Turtles plan an important role in maintaining healthy ocean ecosystems. They support marine food chains and contribute to the balance of underwater habits. Protecting sea turtles also means protecting the ocean for future generations.

Get Involved

Visit the World Wildlife Fund to learn about wildlife conservation or explore NOAA Ocean Service for information about protecting our oceans.
